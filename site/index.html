Playground rules

🪙

Every trade drops a coin

Once the jar graduates, the pool runs in compounding mode: every swap fee goes back into pool liquidity instead of to a fee wallet. More trading, fuller jar.

🔒

Nobody gets backsies

100% of liquidity locked forever. No dev, no team, nobody can take it out. Not even if they ask nicely.

🤖

Snipers pay 10×

The fee opens at 10% and decays to 1% over the first hour. Whoever cuts the line pays the most for it.

Jarold's honesty corner

Jarold can prove

  • Fees that stay make the pool deeper. Same trades, bigger pool. Run it yourself above.
  • A deeper pool flinches less. The same whale dump moves price less.
  • Jarold fills several times faster than a normal graduated pump.fun coin.

Jarold will not promise

  • Number go up. Nobody knows that. Anyone who says they do is lying.
  • A stable price. A fuller jar softens dumps. It doesn't stop them.
  • That people will trade. Higher fees scare some bots off. The sim doesn't model that.
  • That fees compound before graduation. Compounding only exists in the DAMM v2 pool. On the bonding curve itself, fees go to a fee claimer — the chain has no way to route them into the curve.

How Jarold gets built

  1. Bonding curve launchMeteora's Dynamic Bonding Curve sells the first tokens from a 1B supply. No custom contract to audit.
  2. Authorities burned at mintNo mint authority and no metadata authority, from the first block. The supply and the name can never change.
  3. Fee opens at 10%, decays to 1%Exponential, 60 steps over the first hour. Line-cutters fill the jar.
  4. Graduate into a compounding poolA DAMM v2 pool at 1% fee in compounding mode, so the fee goes back into pool liquidity.
  5. Lock 100% of liquidity, permanentlyNot vested, not time-locked — permanent. Nobody has a withdraw path, including us.
  6. Test on devnet firstEvery parameter above is validated by the SDK's own validator and simulated on devnet before anything goes live.
