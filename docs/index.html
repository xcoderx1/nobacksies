The whole idea

A meme coin where the liquidity is locked forever — nobody can take it back out, not even us. You can sell any time. It’s the liquidity that’s locked, not your tokens.
The numbers, the fees we earn and everything we can’t promise live in the nerd corner.

The playground

Jarold

A jar with a padlock and a grudge. Things go in. Nothing comes out. He didn’t make the rules, but he does enforce them.

Takesie Tim

Gives you something, then wants it back ten minutes later. Has “just this once” tattooed somewhere. Every playground has one. So does every token.

Line-Cutter Lenny

A bot with no legs and no shame. Gets to the front of every queue before anyone else has their shoes on. Nobody has worked out how to stop him — Jarold just sighs and writes it down.

Toll Tilda

Runs the tollbooth by the gate. The toll opens steep — 10% in the first seconds, falling to 1% within the hour — and that early toll is hers, less the gate’s own cut. Once the gate opens she keeps half of what the jar earns and the other half stays in. She’ll tell you the exact numbers before you ask; they’re on the board in the nerd corner.
